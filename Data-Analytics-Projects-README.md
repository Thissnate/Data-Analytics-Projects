### 2. [Pixar Films Performance Analysis](./02-pixar-films-analysis)
**Tools:** SQL Server, Power BI  

Analyzed the performance of 28 Pixar films, focusing on box office results, return on investment (ROI), critical ratings, Academy Awards, and trends over time.

**Key Insights:**
- Inside Out 2, Incredibles 2, and Toy Story 4 are among the highest-grossing films

### 3. [Spotify Streaming History Analysis](./spotify-streaming-analysis)
**Tools:** Excel, SQL Server, Power BI  

Analyzed 11+ years of personal Spotify streaming history (148,675 streams) to uncover listening habits, top artists & tracks, skip behaviour, platform usage, and discovery vs loyalty patterns.

**Key Insights:**
- The Beatles are the most listened-to artist (333.1 hours)
- Peak listening occurs late night / early morning (12 AM – 2 AM)
- Android accounts for 93.9% of all streams
- Strong preference for familiar/repeat artists over new ones
- John Mayer and ABBA showed notable growth in 2024
